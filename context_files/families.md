# ECLASS Families

Total entries: 6

---

## 23000000 — Machine element, fastener, fixing, mounting

machine element, fastener, fixing, mounting encompasses a wide range of products used for the manufacturing and maintenance of machines, equipment, and devices, as well as for the construction and assembly of buildings and building equipment. this includes elements such as screws, nuts, bolts, rivets, machine and control elements, wood connection elements, fittings, and anchors, as well as linear motion technology. these parts are essential for the structural integrity and functionality of various systems and are used in industries such as mechanical engineering, plant engineering, vehicle manufacturing, construction, and technical building equipment.

## 27000000 — Electric engineering, automation, process control engineering

this classification encompasses all aspects related to the design, development, implementation, and management of electronic systems and processes aimed at enhancing control and automation in various applications. It includes devices, technologies, and methodologies that facilitate the regulation and monitoring of processes, ensuring efficiency and reliability in operations. The scope covers components and systems used in electrical engineering, emphasizing automation technologies, process control solutions, and related services. This classification differentiates itself by its focus on the integration of electrical components with automated systems, providing solutions for a wide range of industries that require sophisticated control mechanisms and process optimization. The aim is to support advancements in technology that lead to improved productivity and operational effectiveness.

## 35000000 — Semifinished product

prefabricated workpiece (or raw material form) of the simplest form. It usually consists of a single raw material, which has been brought into a basic geometric shape. Semi-finished products are, for example, simple profiles, rods, tubes and plates made of metal, plastic or wood. A semi-finished product is an incompletely finished product that can be further processed into a finished product

## 37000000 — Industrial piping

Segment includes the design and manufacture of piping systems used for the transportation of liquids, gases and sometimes solids in various industrial and commercial applications. This includes pipes, fittings, flanges, valves and supporting control systems. Not included are the pipes and installation elements that are installed in buildings that are not used for industrial applications. These can be found in subject area 22.

---

## 43000000 — Optics

Segment includes products and technologies that deal with the generation, manipulation and detection of light. This includes optical instruments such as microscopes, telescopes, cameras and glasses, optical components such as lenses, prisms and mirrors as well as laser technology and photonic systems. Other products with a focus on electrical engineering are not included in this subject area but are part of segment 27.

---

## 51000000 — Fluid power

area of drive technology, which uses transmission, control, regulation and transfer from energy by a liquid or gaseous pressure fluid to solve drive functions
